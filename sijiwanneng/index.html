图表分析类模板

趋势变化描述

高频考点

第一段：描述图表
As is clearly depicted in the bar chart/line graph, there is a noticeable trend regarding [主题词]. In [起始年份], the number of [图表主语] was [具体数值], which increased/decreased significantly to [结束数值] in [结束年份].

第二段：原因分析
Several factors contribute to this phenomenon. Firstly, [原因一，如：经济水平的提升]. Secondly, [原因二，如：人们观念的改变]. Last but not least, [原因三，如：政策的大力支持].

第三段：结论与展望
Taking into account all the factors discussed above, we can safely conclude that the trend of [主题词] will continue in the forthcoming years. It is of great significance for us to pay close attention to it.

适用：柱状图、折线图 复制模板

比例对比描述

对比类

第一段：概述
The pie chart clearly presents the proportion of [主题] among college students. From the data, we can see that [占比较高的部分] accounts for %, while [占比较低的部分] makes up only %.

第二段：差异分析
The reasons for this difference are manifold. On the one hand, [优势方原因]. On the other hand, [劣势方原因].

第三段：总结
In conclusion, although there are disparities, both sides have their own merits. It is advisable for us to make a rational choice based on our own situations.

适用：饼图 复制模板

议论文写作模板

现象解释型

万能型

第一段：引出话题
Nowadays, [某种社会现象] has become a common topic of discussion. It is widely believed that [观点A], while others argue that [观点B].

第二段：论述观点
From my perspective, I tend to agree with the former/latter opinion. The reasons are as follows. First of all, [理由一]. Furthermore, [理由二].

第三段：总结
In short, [观点A/B] is reasonable. We should take a positive attitude towards it and make good use of it.

适用：观点对比、利弊分析 复制模板

应用文写作指南

建议信

书信类

称呼与开头
Dear [收信人],
I am writing to express my views concerning [建议主题].

正文
There are several suggestions for you to take into consideration. First and foremost, [建议一]. In addition, [建议二]. Last but not least, [建议三].

结尾
I hope you will find these proposals helpful. Looking forward to your reply.

适用：建议、邀请、道歉 复制模板

写作高分技巧

  • 使用连接词增强逻辑性。
  • 避免重复使用相同的词汇。
  • 确保字数在120-180词之间。
  • 书写工整，卷面整洁。
  • 注意时态和语态的正确使用。

万能过渡句

  • What's more / Furthermore
  • Consequently / As a result
  • On the contrary / However
  • It is generally believed that...
  • From my point of view...
